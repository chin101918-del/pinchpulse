What the app uses

Motion sensors

To recognize a pinch or tap, PinchPulse reads your Apple Watch's motion sensors (accelerometer and gyroscope) while a counting session is open. This motion data is processed in real time on the watch, is not saved, and is never transmitted. Sensing stops as soon as the app leaves the screen.

Readings you save

Counted rates (heart rate and respiratory rate), their status, time, and the species you select are stored locally on your Apple Watch and, if you use the companion app, on your paired iPhone. Data moves between your own watch and iPhone directly through Apple's Watch Connectivity. It is not sent to us or to anyone else.

Species settings

Species and their reference ranges, including any custom species you add, are stored on your devices only.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect names, emails, contacts, location, identifiers or usage data.
  • We don't read from or write to Apple Health.
  • We don't use advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking services.
  • We don't sell, rent or share data, because we never receive any.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We receive no payment details. The app only checks with Apple whether the unlock has been purchased on your Apple Account.

Deleting your data

Deleting PinchPulse from your Apple Watch and iPhone removes all of its data from those devices.

Children

PinchPulse is intended for veterinary and clinical users and is not directed at children. It collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date. If the app ever begins to collect data, we'll say so here before that happens.
