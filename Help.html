Getting started

1 · PinchTap index finger and thumb together once for every beat you hear.
2 · DetectThe watch's motion sensors recognize each pinch.
3 · CountThe rate updates live and is checked against the species' normal range.

Frequently asked questions

Does PinchPulse measure the patient's heart rate?

No. The watch never measures the patient. You listen with your stethoscope and pinch once per beat; PinchPulse turns the timing of your pinches into beats or breaths per minute.

Some pinches aren't counted. What can I do?

Keep the watch snug on your wrist, keep your forearm steady, and make each pinch quick and deliberate. Large arm movements pause detection briefly so they aren't mistaken for pinches.

How do I count respiratory rate?

Use Tap mode and tap once per breath. Rates down to 5 breaths per minute are supported; the result is taken from the steady part of the session.

How do species ranges work?

Choose a species before counting. Each reading is labeled Normal, Tachycardia/Tachypnea or Bradycardia/Bradypnea against that species' reference range. You can add custom species on iPhone.

How does pricing work?

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the unlock screen in the app and choose Restore Purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your Apple Watch and paired iPhone. See the Privacy Policy.
